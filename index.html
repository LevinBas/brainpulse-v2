About BrainPulse – Free Browser Trivia & Geography Games

BrainPulse is a free interactive knowledge and geography game platform you can play directly in your browser without registration. Test your general knowledge, sharpen your world map skills, and unlock achievements across 18 unique game modes.

🎯 Trivia & History Modes Play Classic Quiz, Time Attack, Higher or Lower, 4-Clue Dossier, and Chrono-Chain to test your knowledge across science, history, pop culture, and sports.
